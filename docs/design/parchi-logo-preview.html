Parchi logo concepts

Three marks, each on white, paper and navy, with the 16/32/48 px sizes below. No tick mark: Parchi informs, it never approves.

1. The slip

A torn prescription slip with the Rx mark. The indigo bar is a line picked out for review, which is what the product does.

2. The Rx squircle

A rounded indigo app icon with a white Rx monogram. Strongest at small sizes (favicon, PWA home-screen icon).

3. The stub and lens

A tear-off parchi stub with a lens over one line of text: "look closer". The most distinctive, a little busier at small sizes.

Devanagari "पर्ची" is live text here and needs Noto Sans Devanagari. For the real asset it should be converted to outlines. The marks are plain SVG shapes using the brand tokens, so they scale cleanly and can be exported as favicon.svg and PWA icons.
